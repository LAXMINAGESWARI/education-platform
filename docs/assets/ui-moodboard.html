Working with slope

Slope measures how steep a line is. In the form y = mx + b, m is the slope and b is the y-intercept. Keep headings closer to the paragraph they introduce than to the previous section — Gestalt proximity.

When students skim technical material, they look for structural landmarks. A restrained type scale (three primary sizes) reduces clutter while preserving scan anchors.
