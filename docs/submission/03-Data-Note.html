https://github.com/aushakanuj/education-platform

CurioEd did not receive an extract of real students. The generator module states that CBT provided no pupil records, and the early-warning design note records that the sponsor could not confirm historical outcome cohorts. Marks, attendance, names, and classes are produced by education_platform.modules.synthetic from a fixed seed. The same command rebuilds the same school. This note is the data statement for that school and for the rules that read it.

Three shapes are planted on purpose, because a uniform random school makes every rule look the same. Aisha Rahman (S-00097, Grade 8) declines in mathematics: scores 74, 65, 56, 47, 38, mastery 56%, attendance rate 62%, other subjects recorded in the design note between 61% and 77%. Every 17th student has attendance at 70% and marks that are not low. Two sections of one subject differ on a mathematics topic, so a section gap is real and is not collapsed into a single cohort flag.

Rules, recorded with their calibration in modules/at_risk/engine.py: mastery below 60% (synthetic mean mastery 71.3%, SD 8.5; about 11.5% of student–subject rows); a drop of more than 15 points from earlier attempts to the latest three (about 2.9% of groups with history); attendance below 80% (the gap between a 70% cluster and the next mass at 86%). Exact hits on 60, 15, and 80 do not flag. A subject flag and an attendance flag are separate objects. These cuts describe this generated school only.

The data can show that those planted cases fire the intended drivers, and that a teacher and an administrator do not see the same rows. It cannot support a prediction about a real child, a trained model, or a comparison with a live school. Do not treat Al Noor International as a real institution.
